# Student offers for ChatGPT and Codex

Optional offers for extra AI access. Details current as of **October 20, 2026**; check the linked official pages when claiming.

## ChatGPT Plus: four free months

- **Eligibility:** Full-time or part-time college and graduate students at eligible U.S. degree-granting institutions, verified through SheerID.
- **Deadline:** Claim once by **October 31, 2026**. Sign in to the ChatGPT account you want to use.
- New users and existing monthly Plus subscribers billed directly by ChatGPT can qualify. App-store-billed subscriptions are ineligible while billed through the store.
- **Billing:** A payment method is required. After four free monthly billing periods, Plus renews at **$20/month unless canceled**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current monthly period and forfeits any remaining promotional months.

[Official Plus offer details and claim instructions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001493-chatgpt-back-to-school-offer-for-students).

## Codex: $100 in credits

- **Eligibility:** Current students at degree-granting universities in the U.S. or Canada, residing in either country when claiming. Student verification is required; one offer per student.
- **Account:** ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, or Pro. Use your existing ChatGPT account and verify with your university email.
- **Benefit:** $100 in ChatGPT credits for Codex, shown as **2,500 credits**, added automatically after verification. These are not API credits.
- **Expiration:** **12 months after the grant date.**

[Check eligibility and claim Codex credits](https://learn.chatgpt.com/community/students).
